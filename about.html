We build the app Irad uses to talk, trade and pay

Tulpar Technologies is a small company with a development office in Talas. We started with a chat app that could spell Iradi names. Today more than a million people have a Tulpar account.

Our story

Jason Ilyasov moved to Talas in 2016. He grew up in Chicago speaking Iradi at home, and when he came back he found that the apps everyone used could not even spell his neighbours' names. Döölöt became Doolot. Searches for ı, ö and ü found nothing.

In 2018 he hired six graduates of Talas State University, led by Aiperi Kalysbekova, and rented two rooms above a pharmacy on the central avenue. The brief was simple: a free messaging app that works on the phones and connections Iradis actually have, and treats the Iradi language as a first language, not an afterthought.

Tulpar launched in March 2019. Four thousand people downloaded it on the first night. Channels followed in 2020 and Bazar, our classifieds board, in 2021. In 2026 we began Tulpar Pay, so that people who already talk and trade on Tulpar can also pay each other there.

Tulpar Technologies is registered in Delaware, in the United States, because that lets a small Iradi company raise money and sign agreements with the app stores. Every line of Tulpar is written in Talas.

Milestones

  1. 2019

    Tulpar launches

    A free chat app for Android and iPhone, built in Talas, with full support for the Iradi alphabet.

  2. 2020

    Channels

    Anyone can start a channel. Paid channels let writers earn from their readers.

  3. 2021

    Bazar

    A classifieds board inside Tulpar. Sellers post listings and agree deals in chat.

  4. 2023

    140,000 new accounts in a week

    When other services slowed down in October, Tulpar kept working, and our users doubled.

  5. 2025

    Low-data mode

    Tulpar uses up to 60 percent less data on village connections. Monthly users pass 590,000.

  6. 2026

    Tulpar Pay and Tulparcoin

    Wallets in every Tulpar account, 100 free coins for every adult in Irad, and Tulpar points in shops across the country.
