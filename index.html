Everything you do on your phone, in one app

Tulpar started as a chat app that could spell your name. Today it is where Irad talks, buys and sells.

Chat

Messages, voice notes and calls with family in Talas, Toktogul or Moscow. Tulpar writes ı, ö and ü correctly, in names and in search.

  • Low-data mode for village connections
  • Voice notes in Iradi and Russian
  • Groups of up to 1,000 people

Channels

Follow news, football, your school or your street. Anyone can start a channel, and paid channels let writers earn from their readers.

Bazar

Sell a horse, a Lada or last year's apricots. Post a listing with photos, find buyers nearby and agree the deal in chat.

Tulpar Pay

Send money in a chat as easily as a photo. Top up with cash at a Tulpar point, pay for Bazar listings and receive transfers from family abroad without bank fees.
