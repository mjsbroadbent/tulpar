Privacy policy

Tulpar Technologies Inc. ("Tulpar", "we") makes the Tulpar app. This policy explains what we collect, why, and what you can do about it.

What we collect

  • Your mobile number, name and profile photo.
  • Your contacts, if you choose to share them, so you can find friends on Tulpar.
  • Messages and media while they are being delivered. Private messages are deleted from our servers once delivered to every device in the chat.
  • Bazar listings and channel posts, which are public.
  • Wallet balances and payment history, which we need to run Tulpar Pay.
  • Basic technical data: phone model, app version and the network you use.

What we don't do

We don't read your private chats. We don't sell your data. We don't show advertising in private chats.

Where your data is stored

On servers operated by our hosting providers outside the United States. No user data is stored in the United States.

Requests from authorities

We respond only to requests that are lawful, specific and made through proper legal channels. We publish the number of requests we receive, and how many we comply with, twice a year.

Your choices

You can download a copy of your data or delete your account at any time in Settings, then Account.
